## 🎣 INSIGHT #6: HOOK TECHNIQUES

### Hook DNA Formula
```
EFFECTIVE HOA HOOK = [SPECIFIC DETAIL] + [EMOTIONAL TRIGGER] + [UNANSWERED QUESTION]
```

### 7 Loại Hook cho HOA
1. **Villain-First** — "The HOA president knocked at 11pm"
2. **Number Shock** — "$300,000 home. Sold for $3,500."
3. **Absurdity Lead** — "Fined for brown grass. Then arrested."
4. **Victim Identity** — "6,000 miles away, fighting for this country"
5. **Rhetorical Challenge** — "What if your HOA can SELL your house?"
6. **Cold Open Scene** — "The letter was three sentences. It changed everything."
7. **Comedy Bait-and-Switch** — Start funny → "But this ISN'T funny"

## ✍️ INSIGHT #7: KỸ THUẬT VIẾT VO

### Nguyên tắc "1 Câu = 1 Hình"
Mỗi câu script gợi ra 1 hình ảnh rõ ràng cho AI/stock footage.

### Show Don't Tell
- ❌ "He was devastated"
- ✅ "He drove to his address. Someone else's name was on the mailbox."

### Pacing/Rhythm
- **Impact moment**: 2-5 từ ("She was twenty-one.")
- **Setup/Context**: 12-20 từ
- **Narrative flow**: 15-25 từ  
- **MAX**: ≤ 30 từ/câu
- **Pattern**: Long → Medium → SHORT

### Power Words cho HOA niche
```
stolen, destroyed, foreclosed, evicted, fined, seized, 
threatened, bullied, silenced, ignored, violated, 
demanded, forced, controlled, punished, crushed
```

| Phần | Tone |

| Comedy beats | Wry, sarcastic, dark humor |
| Closing | Reflective, empowering |

**Key**: SILENCE ở emotional peak = most powerful. KHÔNG hét.

## 🔀 INSIGHT #9: TRANSITIONS

### Giữa cases/scenes
- **Escalation bridge**: "But that's NOT the worst"
- **Contrast bridge**: "If you thought THAT was bad..."
- **Thematic bridge**: "And it's not just property. It's PEOPLE."
- **Emotional reset**: [joke] → "Okay. Now let me tell you about..."

### Trong single story
- **Time jump**: "Three weeks later..."
- **Location shift**: "Meanwhile, across town..."
- **Perspective shift**: "What the HOA didn't know..."
- **Cliffhanger cut**: "And that's when the letter arrived."
