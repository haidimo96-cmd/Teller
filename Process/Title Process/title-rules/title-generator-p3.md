# 5 PROVEN TITLE FORMULAS

## Formula #1: ⚔️ "Overstep → Payback" (Highest volume, highest views)

```
[HOA/Karen] + [outrageous action] — So I [unexpected retaliation]
```

**Structure**: `INJUSTICE — REVENGE`
**Separator**: Use em dash (—) or ellipsis (...)
**Power words**: "So I", "Then I", "I Made Them"

**Examples:**
- HOA Put 96 Homes on My Land — I Let Them Finish, Then Pulled the... (2.5M views)
- HOA Fined Me $5,000 for a Tree I Didn't Have — So I Planted 50 More
- HOA Board Sold My Driveway — So I Fenced Off Their Only Access Road

**When to use**: Script has clear villain action → hero counter-attack sequence

---

## Formula #2: 🎭 "Identity Reveal" (Highest CTR)

```
[HOA/Karen] + [action against hero] — Didn't Know/Realize [hero's hidden identity/power]
```

**Structure**: `MISTAKE — HIDDEN TRUTH`
**Power words**: "Didn't Know", "Didn't Realize", "Unaware", "Had No Idea"

**Examples:**
- HOA Karen Cut My Lock to Snatch My Jeep — Didn't Know It Belonged to a Police Task Force
- HOA Karen Busted Into My Lake Cabin — Didn't Realize I Was Meeting the State Attorney
- HOA Karen Towed My Car — She Had No Idea I'm the Land Owner

**When to use**: Script has a dramatic reveal of hero's identity, authority, or secret weapon

---

## Formula #3: 💰 "Not a Member" / Injustice (Creates maximum outrage)

```
HOA [illegal/outrageous action] — I'm NOT Even Part of the HOA!
```

**Structure**: `OVERREACH — INJUSTICE REVEAL`
**Power words**: "NOT a Member", "NOT Even Part of", "My OWN Land/Property"

**Examples:**
- HOA Shut Down My BBQ & Forged My Signature — I'm Not Even Part Of The HOA!
- HOA SUED Me For "UNPAID FINES" — I'M NOT EVEN PART OF THE HOA!
- HOA Put A Lien On My 150 Year Old House — I'm NO HOA Member!

**When to use**: Script involves HOA overstepping jurisdiction, targeting non-members, or illegal authority

---

## Formula #4: 💥 "Huge Mistake" (Open-ended cliff-hanger)

```
[HOA/Karen] + [aggressive action]... [HUGE/BIG] Mistake!
```

**Structure**: `ACTION — CONSEQUENCE TEASE`
**Power words**: "HUGE Mistake", "BIG Mistake", "Worst Decision", "Fatal Error"

**Examples:**
- HOA Karen Moved Next to My 150-Year-Old Farm and Tried to Shut It Down — Huge Mistake
- HOA Karen POURED CONCRETE Into My Sewer Line... HUGE Mistake!
- HOA Destroyed My Pickup to Force Me to Join... BIG Mistake

**When to use**: Script's payoff is so dramatic that just teasing "mistake" creates enough curiosity

---

## Formula #5: 🔢 "Specific Numbers" (Credibility + stakes)

```
[Story] + [specific number: money, quantity, years, count]
```

**Structure**: `CONFLICT + CONCRETE STAKES`
**Power words**: "$[exact amount]", "[X] homes", "[X] years", "Judge awarded $[amount]"

**Examples:**
- HOA Put 96 Homes on My Land (2.5M views)
- I Sued Them For $3,000,000! (474K views)
- HOA Karen Sold Deployed Soldier's Home — Judge ENDED the HOA Forever $1.5M Judgment

**When to use**: Script involves financial stakes, property quantities, or legal judgments

# POWER WORDS REFERENCE

## Villain Labels (use at START of title):
- "HOA Karen" — most viral, signals entitled antagonist
- "HOA President" — signals authority abuse
- "HOA Board" — signals group villainy
- "My HOA" — personal, relatable

## Action Verbs (villain actions, ALL CAPS optional for emphasis):
- TOWED, DEMOLISHED, FINED, SUED, SOLD, STOLE, BLOCKED, DESTROYED
- "Cut my lock", "Forged my signature", "Put a lien on"

## Twist/Payoff Signals (use at END of title):
- "Didn't Know...", "Didn't Realize..."
- "So I...", "Then I..."
- "HUGE Mistake!", "BIG Mistake!"
- "I'm NOT a Member!"
- "Instant Arrest!", "Instant Regret!"

## Emotional Amplifiers:
- Elderly/Veteran: "82-Year-Old Veteran", "Disabled Vet", "Elderly Widow"
- Family: "My Dead Father's Car", "My Wife's Garden", "Inherited House"
- Property: "My OWN Land", "On MY Driveway", "MY Private Property"

# FORMATTING RULES

1. **Length**: 60–80 characters ideal (YouTube truncates at ~100 chars on mobile)
2. **Capitalization**: Title Case for most words; ALL CAPS for 1-2 key power words only
3. **Punctuation**:
   - Em dash (—) to separate setup from payoff
   - Ellipsis (...) for cliff-hangers
   - Exclamation mark (!) for "Huge Mistake!" endings
   - No periods (.)
4. **No clickbait lies** — title must reflect actual story content
5. **Truncation strategy**: If title is long, ensure it cuts at the most curiosity-inducing point
6. **No emojis in title** — keep it professional
7. **No subreddit tags** (r/MaliciousCompliance) — unless channel brand includes Reddit

# GENERATION PROCESS

When generating titles from a final script:

## Step 1: Extract Key Elements
Read the final script and identify:
- **Villain**: Who is the antagonist? (Name, role, label)
- **Villain's worst action**: What's the most outrageous thing they did?
- **Hero's secret weapon**: What does the hero have that the villain doesn't know?
- **The twist**: What's the surprise reveal?
- **Numbers**: Any specific amounts ($, years, quantities)?
- **Emotional anchor**: What object/person makes this personal? (dead father's car, grandmother's garden)
- **The payoff**: How does the villain lose?

## Step 2: Generate 5 Titles
Generate exactly **5 title options**, one for each formula:
1. **Option A**: Formula #1 (Overstep → Payback)
2. **Option B**: Formula #2 (Identity Reveal)
3. **Option C**: Formula #3 or #4 (Injustice / Huge Mistake)
4. **Option D**: Formula #5 or Combo (Numbers / Mixed)
5. **Option E**: Wild card — most creative/unusual angle

## Step 3: Analyze Each Title (Psychology Deep-Dive)
For each title, provide:

### Scoring:
- **Curiosity score** (1-10): How badly does viewer NEED to click?
- **Clarity score** (1-10): Can viewer understand the conflict in 2 seconds?
- **Emotion score** (1-10): Does it trigger outrage/empathy/satisfaction?
- **Gap Level** (1-5): What curiosity gap level? (Target: 3 or 4)
- **Truncation safety**: What does mobile viewer see at ~70 chars?

### Psychology Breakdown:
- **Primary trigger**: Which of the 12 emotional triggers is dominant?
- **Secondary trigger(s)**: Supporting emotion(s)?
- **Linguistic techniques**: Which LT-1 through LT-8 are present?
- **Two-clause analysis**: What does Clause 1 do? What does Clause 2 do? Relationship type?
- **Power words used**: Which power/reveal words are present?
- **Viewer's unspoken question**: What specific question forms in the viewer's mind?

## Step 4: Run Professional Checklist
Run each title through the **Professional Title Creator Checklist** (sections A-E, see Part 2).
Only recommend titles that pass:
- ALL items in section A (Emotional Architecture)
- ALL items in section B (Curiosity Gap)
- ≥5 of 8 items in section C (Linguistic Craft)
- ALL items in section D (Two-Clause Structure)
- ALL items in section E (Technical)

## Step 5: Recommend Top Pick
Choose the best title and explain why. Consider:
- Which formula best matches this specific story's strongest element?
- Which title creates the most powerful "curiosity gap"?
- Which triggers the strongest emotional combination?
- Which would stand out in a feed full of similar HOA content?
- Which has the best truncation point on mobile?

# ANTI-PATTERNS — NEVER DO

1. ❌ **Generic titles**: "HOA Story" / "Crazy HOA" / "You Won't Believe This"
2. ❌ **Spoil the ending**: Don't reveal the full payoff — tease it
3. ❌ **Too long**: Titles over 100 chars get cut badly on mobile
4. ❌ **No villain**: Every title needs a clear antagonist
5. ❌ **No stakes**: Title must convey what's at risk
6. ❌ **Emoji spam**: No emojis in title
7. ❌ **All caps everything**: Only capitalize 1-2 KEY words for emphasis
8. ❌ **Misleading**: Title must match actual story content
