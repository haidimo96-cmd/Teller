## Curiosity Gap — The 5 Levels

The "curiosity gap" is the space between what the title REVEALS and what the viewer NEEDS TO KNOW. Too small = no click. Too large = confusion.

| Level | Gap Size | Title Reveals | Title Hides | Click Likelihood | Example |
|-------|----------|---------------|-------------|------------------|---------|
| 1 | ❌ None | Everything | Nothing | Very Low — no reason to click | "HOA Karen Fined Me And Lost" |
| 2 | ⚠️ Small | Conflict + outcome hint | Method/details | Low — predictable | "HOA Fined Me So I Got Them Back" |
| 3 | ✅ **Optimal** | Conflict + stakes | Method + outcome | **Highest** — irresistible | "HOA Fined Me $5K — So I Planted 50 More..." |
| 4 | ✅ Strong | Conflict only | Everything else | High — but might confuse | "HOA Karen Made One Huge Mistake..." |
| 5 | ❌ Too Big | Almost nothing | Everything | Low — too vague | "You Won't Believe What This HOA Did" |

**Target**: Always aim for **Level 3** (Optimal) or **Level 4** (Strong).

### Curiosity Gap Checklist:
- [ ] Title reveals the CONFLICT (what's at stake)? → **YES required**
- [ ] Title reveals the VILLAIN (who did it)? → **YES required**
- [ ] Title hides the METHOD (how hero wins)? → **YES required**
- [ ] Title hides the FULL OUTCOME (exact resolution)? → **YES required**
- [ ] Title creates a QUESTION in viewer's mind? → **YES required**
- [ ] The hidden question is SPECIFIC (not vague)? → **YES required**

## Two-Clause Analysis — The Anatomy of a Two-Part Title

Most viral HOA titles have **TWO clauses** separated by an em dash (—) or ellipsis (...).
Each clause has a distinct function, and the RELATIONSHIP between them drives the click.

### Clause Roles:

| | Clause 1 (Setup) | Clause 2 (Payoff/Hook) |
|---|---|---|
| **Function** | Establish the CONFLICT | Create the CURIOSITY |
| **Contains** | Villain + Action + Stakes | Twist / Reveal / Consequence |
| **Emotion** | OUTRAGE or FEAR | CURIOSITY or SATISFACTION |
| **Viewer reaction** | "That's so unfair!" | "Wait... what happens next?!" |
| **Length** | Usually longer (60-70% of title) | Usually shorter (30-40%) |

### 6 Relationship Types Between Clauses:

| Relationship | Clause 1 | Connector | Clause 2 | Example |
|-------------|----------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Cause → Effect** | Villain acts | "So I" / "Then I" | Hero retaliates | "HOA Fined Me — So I Fenced Off Their Road" |
| **Action → Hidden Truth** | Villain acts | "Didn't Know" | Reality revealed | "HOA Towed My Car — Didn't Know I'm a Cop" |
| **Claim → Contradiction** | Villain claims | "But" / "—" | Truth exposed | "HOA Says I Owe $50K — I'm NOT a Member" |
| **Action → Consequence** | Villain acts | "..." / "—" | Tease outcome | "HOA Karen Cut My Fence... HUGE Mistake" |
| **Accumulation → Snap** | Multiple offenses | "Finally" / "Until" | Hero breaks | "3 Years of HOA Fines — Finally I Snapped" |
| **Setup → Irony** | Normal situation | "—" | Ironic twist | "HOA Poisoned My Lake — It Was Their Water Supply" |

### Two-Clause Quality Test:
- [ ] **Clause 1 standalone**: Does Clause 1 alone create an emotional reaction? (Outrage/Fear)
- [ ] **Clause 2 standalone**: Does Clause 2 alone raise a question? (Curiosity)
- [ ] **Together > Apart**: Are the two clauses stronger TOGETHER than separately?
- [ ] **Tension between clauses**: Is there CONTRAST or SURPRISE between them?
- [ ] **No redundancy**: Do the clauses provide DIFFERENT information (not repeat)?

## Power Words & Reveal Words — Complete Reference

### Power Words (Trigger immediate emotional response):

| Category | Words | Emotion Triggered |
|----------|-------|-------------------|
| **Violation** | STOLE, DESTROYED, DEMOLISHED, INVADED, TRESPASSED, CONFISCATED | Moral Outrage |
| **Aggression** | SCREAMED, THREATENED, FORCED, BULLIED, HARASSED, ATTACKED | Fear + Anger |
| **Legal/Authority** | SUED, ARRESTED, FINED, EVICTED, CONDEMNED, LIEN | High Stakes |
| **Personal** | MY (land/house/car), DEAD FATHER'S, INHERITED, DISABLED, ELDERLY | Empathy |
| **Scale** | ENTIRE, EVERY, ALL, 96 HOMES, $3,000,000, 150 YEARS | Magnitude |
| **Consequence** | ENDED, DESTROYED, COLLAPSED, BANKRUPT, FIRED, VOTED OUT | Satisfaction |

### Reveal Words (Signal hidden information is coming):

| Category | Words | Function |
|----------|-------|----------|
| **Ignorance** | Didn't Know, Didn't Realize, Had No Idea, Unaware, Forgot | Signals villain's blindspot |
| **Discovery** | Turns Out, Discovered, Found Out, Revealed, Exposed | Signals truth reveal |
| **Consequence** | HUGE Mistake, BIG Mistake, Fatal Error, Worst Decision | Signals coming punishment |
| **Transition** | So I, Then I, Until I, That's When I, Finally | Signals hero's counter-action |
| **Identity** | I'm the Owner, I'm a Cop, I'm a Judge, I'm Not a Member | Signals power flip |

# PROFESSIONAL TITLE CREATOR CHECKLIST

> Use this checklist AFTER generating each title to verify quality.

## A. Emotional Architecture (Must pass ALL):
- [ ] **Primary trigger identified?** (Which of the 12 triggers is dominant?)
- [ ] **Secondary trigger present?** (At least 1 supporting trigger?)
- [ ] **Emotional progression?** (Clause 1 emotion ≠ Clause 2 emotion — e.g., Outrage → Curiosity)
- [ ] **Viewer can PROJECT themselves?** (Would viewer think "that could be ME"?)

## B. Curiosity Gap (Must pass ALL):
- [ ] **Gap level = 3 or 4?** (Not too small, not too big)
- [ ] **Specific question formed?** (Viewer knows WHAT they don't know)
- [ ] **Answer NOT in title?** (Method/outcome hidden)
- [ ] **Irresistible?** Would viewer REGRET not clicking?

## C. Linguistic Craft (Must pass ≥5 of 8):
- [ ] **Contrast/Juxtaposition?** (LT-1: Weak vs. Strong, Sacred vs. Insulting)
- [ ] **Specific detail?** (LT-2: Number, name, object — not vague)
- [ ] **Physical/sensory verb?** (LT-3: RIPPED, TORE, DEMOLISHED — not "removed")
- [ ] **First person?** (LT-4: "I", "My", "Me" — personal stakes)
- [ ] **Truncation-safe?** (LT-5: If cut, still creates curiosity)
- [ ] **Status reversal signal?** (LT-7: WHO rises, WHO falls)
- [ ] **Temporal cue?** (LT-8: "Then", "So", "After", "For 3 Years")
- [ ] **No abstract verbs?** (No "got", "did", "was" as main verbs)

## D. Two-Clause Structure (Must pass ALL):
- [ ] **Clause 1 = Conflict?** (Villain + Action + Stakes)
- [ ] **Clause 2 = Hook?** (Twist / Reveal / Consequence tease)
- [ ] **Clauses create TENSION?** (Contrast or surprise between them)
- [ ] **No redundancy?** (Each clause adds NEW information)
- [ ] **Correct separator?** (Em dash — for hard cuts, Ellipsis ... for trailing mystery)

## E. Technical (Must pass ALL):
- [ ] **60-80 characters?** (Mobile-safe length)
- [ ] **≤2 ALL CAPS words?** (Emphasis, not shouting)
- [ ] **No spoilers?** (Outcome teased, not revealed)
- [ ] **Matches actual story?** (No clickbait lies)
- [ ] **Would beat competitors?** (Compare against top HOA titles in same formula)
