We’ve all seen them. The "Free Course" that promises a million dollars. The "Zero Down" real estate deals. But in the world of content creation, $0 usually carries the heaviest price tag of all. Today, we're pulling back the curtain on the economy of attention and the literal cost of being 'free'.

[CHAPTER 1: THE GEAR TRAP]

Most beginners think they need the $5,000 Sony setup. They spend months researching instead of shooting. That lost time? That's your first major cost. If you value your time at just $25/hour, three months of research has already cost you $4,000 in opportunity cost.

"The best camera is the one you already have, because it's the only one that doesn't cost you a second more of procrastination."

When we talk about production value, we often mistake expensive for professional. But professional is a mindset, not a receipt from B&H Photo. The real hidden cost is the mental weight of unexecuted ideas.
